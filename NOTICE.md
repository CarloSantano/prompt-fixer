# Provenance

Prompt Fixer instructions and offline helpers are maintained for Carlo Santo
v3.0. Research provenance and distinctions between documented guidance,
practitioner patterns and local design decisions are recorded in
[research notes](skills/prompt-fixer/references/research-notes.md).

Links to external documentation and repositories do not relicense their source,
imply affiliation, or establish our performance claims. Historical AI responses
and generated component fixtures are retained as evidence, with their stated
evaluation limitations. No external prompt library is bundled wholesale.
