# Research provenance and limits

Survey date: 2026-11-21. This registry is an original synthesis for natural-language, real-business work. It draws on selected open prompt libraries, primary lab material and practical agent workflow collections. It is not an audit of every prompt, a ranking of the world's largest libraries, a benchmark result or a guarantee of automatic invocation.

The process-owner, sales-stage, demand, maintenance, architecture-routing and point-of-use teaching lenses are our contextual adaptations to the user's stated needs, not separately proven prompt techniques. Architecture routing preserves ambition and selects for quality, delivery and total cost rather than automatically minimizing complexity or tokens. Coverage is qualitative; recurrence was not counted across every entry. Existing Reddit/developer discussions supplied leads and failure reports in the earlier research, not technical authority.

## Evidence and updates

Separate structural validity, explicit behavioral tests, untagged host selection and business performance. A passing example does not establish guaranteed activation or a quantified success gain. Record test inputs, observed outputs, version, actual checks and limitations. Compare real work later using requirement fidelity, needless questions, corrective turns, usable completion, time and cost when observed; do not silently collect private cross-client material or start paid experiments.

This plugin uses selected instructions in the current host. It does not intercept all messages, train the underlying model, create new permissions or guarantee another host will expose the same capabilities. Changes to the registry require an authorized update and regression checks.
